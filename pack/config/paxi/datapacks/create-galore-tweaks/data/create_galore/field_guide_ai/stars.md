# The Road to the Stars (stars)
Every way off the planet needs a Stellar Core; the core needs one prize from each big branch of the world.

## stars/why | Why Space Is Locked
- Rockets and space ships are the end of the road. Every way off the planet needs a Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core); a core needs one prize from each big branch of the world.
- Six prizes:
  - Immortal Embryo (see stars/abyss): deep sea
  - 4 Fiery Ingots (see stars/twilight): Twilight Forest
  - 4 Enchanted Gravitite (see stars/aether): the Aether
  - Nether Star (see stars/nether): the Nether
  - 4 Dragonskin (see stars/end): the End
  - 2 Circuit Boards (see stars/industry): heavy industry
- Gates all three ways up: Northstar Rocket Station (see space/rocket_building), Dimensional Drive (see space/aeronautics_bridge), Stellar Navigator.
- One core per vehicle is enough: each block is placed once and reused trip after trip. Friends can share one rocket.
- No dead ends: nothing on the list comes from space; nothing on Earth needs a space item to work. Every prize can be found again: bosses respawn or have more lairs; ores keep generating further out. Titanium is made on Earth by sequenced assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly).
- Checklist: Advancements tab "Road to the Stars" ticks off each prize as picked up, then the core, then your first trip off Earth.
- Bosses can all be beaten with Create builds: Fighting with Machines (see stars/machine_fights).
- Related: The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), Prize: The Abyss (see stars/abyss), Prize: Twilight Forest (see stars/twilight), Prize: The Aether (see stars/aether), Prize: The Nether (see stars/nether), Prize: The End (see stars/end), Prize: Industry (see stars/industry), Reaching Space (see space/reaching_space).

## stars/stellar_core | The Stellar Core
- Stellar Core [create_galore_bridge:stellar_core]: heart of any craft that leaves Earth. Made in a 5x5 Mechanical Crafter grid (see glossary/mechanical_crafting). Press R over it in JEI (see start_here/tools) for layout. shows item create_galore_bridge:stellar_core
- Grid: 1 Immortal Embryo (centre), 1 Nether Star, 1 Hardened Precision Mechanism, 4 Fiery Ingots, 4 Enchanted Gravitite, 4 Dragonskin, 2 TFMG Circuit Boards, 4 Titanium Plates.
- Why each: Gravitite lifts the craft, fiery metal survives the burn, Nether Star powers it, Dragonskin holds out the void, Embryo keeps crew alive, circuit boards steer. Lose any one and the craft never leaves the ground.
- Rocket Station [northstar:rocket_station]: now built around a Stellar Core, plus a Targeting Computer, circuits, titanium. See Building a Rocket (see space/rocket_building). shows item northstar:rocket_station
- Dimensional Drive [cnbridge:dimensional_drive]: a Stellar Core ringed by Titanium Ingots and Precision Mechanisms; takes an Aeronautics ship (see flight/airships) to the planets. shows item cnbridge:dimensional_drive
- Stellar Navigator: a Stellar Core, Titanium, Levitite Blend, glass, redstone. shows recipe northstar_aeronautics_compat:stellar_navigator
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), Flying Ships to Space (see space/aeronautics_bridge).

## stars/abyss | Prize: The Abyss
- Immortal Embryo [alexscaves:immortal_embryo]: always dropped by a Hullbreaker, giant of the Abyssal Chasm. Keeps the crew alive out there. shows item alexscaves:immortal_embryo
- Where: Abyssal Chasm is an Alex's Caves (see exploration/alexs_caves) biome deep under ocean, pitch black and crushing. A Cave Biome Map [alexscaves:cave_map] points to one. Go down in a submarine (see ocean/submarines): carries air, light, weapons.
- How: Hullbreakers spawn there naturally, rarely. Underwater Mines and Depth Charges hit it from the safety of your hull. More will spawn if you need another.
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core).

## stars/twilight | Prize: Twilight Forest
- Fiery Ingot [twilightforest:fiery_ingot]: Fiery Blood or Fiery Tears plus an iron ingot. Only fiery metal survives a launch's heat. shows item twilightforest:fiery_ingot
- Fiery Blood: Hydra in the Fire Swamp drops 7 to 10; reached after the Labyrinth. Fiery Tears: Ur-Ghast atop the Dark Tower drops 1 to 5. One Hydra covers the core.
- How: Twilight bosses drop into a chest where they started. Each lair holds one boss, but every new Fire Swamp or Dark Tower has another. Cannons (see industry/cannons) and autocannons work well on the Hydra's heads. A Hydra Compass finds the lair: see Boss Compasses (see stars/boss_compasses).
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), Twilight Forest (see exploration/twilight_forest).

## stars/aether | Prize: The Aether
- Enchanted Gravitite [aether:enchanted_gravitite]: Gravitite Ore enchanted at an Aether Altar. Falls upward, what a rocket wants. shows item aether:enchanted_gravitite
- Where: Gravitite Ore is a rare ore in Aether Holystone; needs an iron-tier pickaxe (Zanite [aether:zanite_pickaxe] works). Mine, then enchant at an Altar fuelled with Ambrosium [aether:ambrosium_shard]. No boss needed; the Aether goes on forever.
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), The Aether (see exploration/aether).

## stars/nether | Prize: The Nether
- Nether Star [minecraft:nether_star]: dropped by the Wither; a power source that never runs dry. shows item minecraft:nether_star
- How: collect three Wither Skeleton Skulls from Nether Fortresses, summon the Wither somewhere you don't mind wrecking. Big Cannons (see industry/cannons) and autocannons work well from a bunker. Summon again for more stars.
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), The Nether (see exploration/nether), Bosses (see exploration/bosses).

## stars/end | Prize: The End
- Dragonskin [irons_spellbooks:dragonskin]: Ender Dragon drops 6 to 10 in this pack. A hull that has flown the void can hold it out. shows item irons_spellbooks:dragonskin
- How: reach the End through a Stronghold portal and fight the dragon. Ranged builds help: arrows or autocannon (see industry/cannons) shots break the healing crystals. To fight again, place four End Crystals on the exit portal to bring the dragon back.
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), The End (see exploration/the_end), Bosses (see exploration/bosses).

## stars/boss_compasses | Boss Compasses
- Structures are spread out in Create Galore, so a lair can be a long walk. One compass per Twilight boss on the way to the Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core). Hydra drops the Fiery Blood the core needs; Twilight Forest only lets you reach it after beating the Naga, Lich, Minoshroom, in that order.
- Each compass = Compass plus drops you will have by then:
  - Naga Compass: finds Naga Courtyard. Compass, Raven Feather, Torchberries.
  - Lich Compass: finds Lich Tower. Compass and 2 Naga Scales.
  - Minoshroom Compass: finds Minotaur Labyrinth. Compass and Exanimate Essence (Lich drops it).
  - Hydra Compass: finds Hydra's Lair in the Fire Swamp. Compass, Raw Meef, a Maze Slimeball from the Labyrinth.
  - shows recipe create_galore:boss_compass/naga_compass
  - shows recipe create_galore:boss_compass/lich_compass
  - shows recipe create_galore:boss_compass/minoshroom_compass
  - shows recipe create_galore:boss_compass/hydra_compass
- Using: right-click anywhere in the Twilight Forest. Chat shows X and Z of the nearest lair and distance; then the needle points there like a lodestone compass. Searches up to 1600 blocks. Right-click again later to search from where you are now. Outside the Twilight Forest it only tells you where it works.
- Watch out: finds the nearest lair, even one you already cleared; head past it and search again.
- Tip: Twilight's Magic Map [twilightforest:magic_map] and Maze Map [twilightforest:maze_map] still work and show the area around the lair.
- No compass needed for other prize bosses: Wither is summoned; Ender Dragon waits past the Stronghold portal (an Eye of Ender finds the Stronghold); Hullbreaker roams the Abyssal Chasm biome, which a Cave Biome Map [alexscaves:cave_map] finds.
- Related: Prize: Twilight Forest (see stars/twilight), Fighting with Machines (see stars/machine_fights), Twilight Forest (see exploration/twilight_forest), Bosses (see exploration/bosses).

## stars/industry | Prize: Industry
- Circuit Board [tfmg:circuit_board]: the TFMG board, the brain that steers. Core needs two. shows item tfmg:circuit_board
- Chain: refine crude oil into plastic -> Plastic Sheets and green dye make an Empty Board -> deploy a gold plate on it -> etch with Sulfuric Acid in a Chemical Vat -> finish with sequenced assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly): Capacitors, Resistors, a Transistor. Each step is in JEI.
- Needs a working refinery and chemical plant; build these before planning a trip. See Oil (see industry/tfmg_oil).
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), The Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), TFMG Oil and Fuels (see industry/tfmg_oil), Electricity (see industry/electricity).

## stars/machine_fights | Fighting with Machines
- Four prizes come from bosses; every one can be beaten by a Create build (machines don't miss, don't get scared, can be rebuilt).
- Tools: Big Cannons (see industry/cannons) and autocannons for range; Underwater Mines and Depth Charges for the sea; Potato Cannons for cheap firepower; trains, ships, subs to carry it all.
- Tips: build from the safety of a vehicle or bunker; bring the build to the boss on a train, ship or submarine. Tip: bosses do not scale with player count here, so bring a friend.
- Related: Why Space Is Locked (see stars/why), Bosses (see exploration/bosses), Submarines (see ocean/submarines).
