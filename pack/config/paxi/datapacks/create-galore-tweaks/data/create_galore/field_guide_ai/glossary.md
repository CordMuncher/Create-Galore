# Words to Know (glossary)
Short definitions of jargon the book uses unexplained, mostly Create and survival. Purple words in the book link to these entries; each lists where the word matters.

## glossary/index | A to Z
- Index of entries: Assemble (see glossary/assemble); Bearing (see glossary/bearing); Body Temperature (see glossary/body_temperature); Bogey (see glossary/bogey); Boiler (Steam) (see glossary/boiler); Bulk Processing (see glossary/bulk_processing); Canonical Item (see glossary/canonical_item); Chunk (see glossary/chunk); Compacting (see glossary/compacting); Config (see glossary/config); Copycat (see glossary/copycat); Crushed Ore (see glossary/crushed_ore); Curios (see glossary/curios); Datapack (see glossary/datapack); Encased (see glossary/encased); Fan Catalyst (see glossary/fan_catalyst); FE (Forge Energy) (see glossary/fe); Filter Slot (see glossary/filter_slot); Goggles (see glossary/goggles); Grace Period (see glossary/grace); Haunting (see glossary/haunting); Heated (see glossary/heated); Insulation (see glossary/insulation); JEI (see glossary/jei); Kinetic Network (see glossary/kinetic_network); Mana (see glossary/mana); mB (Millibuckets) (see glossary/mb); Mechanical Crafting (see glossary/mechanical_crafting); Mixing (see glossary/mixing); Multiblock (see glossary/multiblock); Overstressed (see glossary/overstressed); Passive Heater (see glossary/passive_heater); Ponder (see glossary/ponder); Purified Water (see glossary/purified_water); Redstone Link Frequency (see glossary/redstone_frequency); RPM (see glossary/rpm); Saturation (see glossary/saturation); Sequenced Assembly (see glossary/sequenced_assembly); Smog (see glossary/smog); SU (Stress Units) (see glossary/su); Sub-level (see glossary/sub_level); Super Glue (see glossary/super_glue); Superheated (see glossary/superheated); Tags (see glossary/tags); Tick (see glossary/tick); Value Panel (see glossary/value_panel); Washing (see glossary/washing); Y Level (see glossary/y_level).

## glossary/assemble | Assemble
- Assemble: turn placed blocks into one moving contraption, train, ship or rocket; Disassemble turns it back into blocks. Glue decides what comes along; movers like bearings assemble their own.
- Trains: Train Station Assembly Mode, disassemble only at a station (see transport/trains). Ships: Physics Assembler (see flight/ship_building).
- Matters: Contraptions (see create_basics/contraptions); Trains (see transport/trains); Building a Ship (see flight/ship_building); Building a Rocket (see space/rocket_building).

## glossary/bearing | Bearing
- Bearing: a mover that spins the structure glued to its face as a contraption. Reverse the input and it turns the other way.
- Types: Mechanical (drills, turntables) (see create_basics/contraptions); Windmill (see create_basics/power_sources); Propeller (see flight/propellers); Swivel (sub-ship) (see flight/ship_building).

## glossary/body_temperature | Body Temperature
- Body temperature: Cold Sweat's stat with its own HUD icon; drifts toward world temperature, too far = damage and cannot sleep (see survival/temperature). Insulated armor slows the drift; a fire, burner or Hearth warms you (see survival/temperature_gear).
- Matters: Temperature (see survival/temperature); Insulation and Heaters (see survival/temperature_gear); Your First Days (see start_here/first_days).

## glossary/bogey | Bogey
- Bogey: the wheel unit a train car rides on; placement, small/large, cycling designs (see transport/trains). Blocks glued on top become the car. Steam 'n' Rails adds more designs and track gauges (see transport/train_addons).

## glossary/boiler | Boiler (Steam)
- Create boiler: Fluid Tanks of water heated from below with Steam Engines attached; min 4 tanks; power level per heat (see create_basics/power_sources).
- Cold Sweat's Boiler is a different block, an area heater (see survival/temperature_gear).
- Matters: Power Sources (see create_basics/power_sources); Blaze Burner and Heat (see create_basics/heat); Insulation and Heaters (see survival/temperature_gear).

## glossary/bulk_processing | Bulk Processing
- Bulk processing (fan processing): what an Encased Fan does to items in its air; the block the air passes through decides the type, fan speed only changes range (see create_basics/fans).
- Matters: Encased Fans (see create_basics/fans); Dragons Plus Fan Effects (see industry/dragons_plus); Create Garnished (see food/garnished); Conduit Cages (see ocean/conduits).

## glossary/canonical_item | Canonical Item
- Canonical item: the one version of a metal the pack picks when several mods add it; others convert 1:1 in a crafting grid (see materials/shared_metals). Steel TFMG's, bronze Alloyed's, lead Create Nuclear's.
- Matters: Shared Metals (see materials/shared_metals); One Metal, Many Mods (see connections/metals).

## glossary/chunk | Chunk
- World stored in chunks: columns 16 by 16 blocks, top to bottom. F3 + G shows borders.
- Smog, ore veins, oil deposits count per chunk. Chunks far from players unload and their machines stop; chunk loaders keep them running (see industry/power_loader). Trains still travel through unloaded chunks (see transport/trains).
- Matters: Chunk Loaders (see industry/power_loader); Pollution (see survival/pollution); Ore Excavation (see industry/ore_excavation); Trains (see transport/trains).

## glossary/compacting | Compacting
- Compacting: Mechanical Press over a Basin (see create_basics/processing).
- Pack examples: Cactus -> purified water + green dye (see survival/thirst); chocolate -> bars (see food/sweets_and_drinks); Yellowcake (see industry/nuclear). JEI lists them.

## glossary/config | Config
- Config: a mod's settings file; whoever runs the game or server can change it. "Set by config" = value can differ in your world.
- Pack tunes several, e.g. a gentler Cold Sweat. Files in the config folder; some open from the Mods menu.
- Matters: Stress (see create_basics/stress); Hulls and Pressure (see ocean/hulls_and_pressure); Seasons (see survival/seasons); Chunk Loaders (see industry/power_loader).

## glossary/copycat | Copycat
- Copycat: block that takes the look of any block applied to it. Copycats+ supplies them here; Create: Connected's own are switched off (see home/create_decor). Create Propulsion has copycat wings (see flight/propulsion).
- Matters: Create Decor (see home/create_decor); Thrusters and Engines (see flight/propulsion); Ship Hardware (see flight/ship_hardware).

## glossary/crushed_ore | Crushed Ore
- Crushed ore (e.g. Crushed Raw Iron): output of Crushing Wheels or Millstone; smelt/blast it or fan-wash it for nuggets and byproduct chance. Crush ore blocks, not raw items (75% second crushed ore) (see create_basics/crushing).
- Matters: Ore Doubling (see materials/ore_doubling); Crushing and Ore Doubling (see create_basics/crushing); Ore Guide (see materials/ore_guide).

## glossary/curios | Curios
- Curios: extra equipment slots beside armor; gear worn there counts on top of armor. Artifacts, Iron's Spells, Critters and Companions fill them (see exploration/gear).
- Matters: Gear and Curios (see exploration/gear); Iron's Spellbooks (see exploration/irons_spellbooks); Diving Gear (see ocean/diving).

## glossary/datapack | Datapack
- Datapack: bundle of data, not code (recipes, loot, world generation); loads with the world.
- Pack loads its own through Paxi; this book is one. Cataclysm x Create and Seasonal Integration are datapacks, so is the Elytra switch.
- Matters: Getting Around (see transport/getting_around); Cataclysm x Create (see exploration/cataclysm_create); Seasons (see survival/seasons).

## glossary/encased | Encased
- Encased: shaft, cogwheel or belt hidden in a casing; right-click with Andesite or Brass Casing; keeps working. Parts placed after encasing need the side toggled with a wrench (see create_basics/transmission). Casings are also the base of many machines (see create_basics/materials).
- Matters: Transmission (see create_basics/transmission); Core Materials (see create_basics/materials); Wrench and Goggles (see create_basics/tools_wrench); Create Decor (see home/create_decor).

## glossary/fan_catalyst | Fan Catalyst
- Fan catalyst: what an Encased Fan blows through (water, fire, lava, soul fire; Blaze Burner) (see create_basics/fans). Add-ons: powder snow, Liquid Dye (see industry/dragons_plus); Enriching Fire (see create_basics/fans); Conduit Cages (see ocean/conduits).

## glossary/fe | FE (Forge Energy)
- FE: electricity many mods share. Create itself runs on rotation, not FE; they meet only through converters: Alternator (from 32 RPM) and Electric Motor (see industry/power_compared) (see industry/electricity).
- Matters: Electricity (see industry/electricity); Rotation vs Electricity (see industry/power_compared); Power Across Mods (see connections/power).

## glossary/filter_slot | Filter Slot
- Filter slot: small slot on the face of a brass funnel, tunnel, Smart Chute and many machines; an item or filter in it chooses what passes (List/Attribute Filters) (see create_basics/item_logistics). On a machine where two recipes fit, an item in its filter slot picks one (see create_basics/processing).
- Matters: Moving Items (see create_basics/item_logistics); Press, Mixer and Basin (see create_basics/processing); Enchantment Industry (see industry/enchanting).

## glossary/goggles | Goggles
- Goggles: Engineer's Goggles, worn on head, show info on the block you look at (stress, capacity, speed, tank/basin contents) (see create_basics/tools_wrench); boiler power level (see create_basics/stress). Many add-on blocks give extra readouts through them. Aviator's Goggles work the same (see flight/airships).
- Matters: Wrench and Goggles (see create_basics/tools_wrench); Stress (see create_basics/stress); Airships (see flight/airships).

## glossary/grace | Grace Period
- Grace: Cold Sweat's short immunity to temperature damage after spawn or death; ~5 minutes when spawning in snow, then no warm gear = freezing (see survival/temperature). Use it to find shelter, a campfire, insulation.
- Matters: Temperature (see survival/temperature); Your First Days (see start_here/first_days); What to Expect (see start_here/difficulty).

## glossary/haunting | Haunting
- Haunting: fan processing through soul fire, soul campfire or a Lit Blaze Burner (see create_basics/fans). Create Garnished adds many recipes (see food/garnished); orange -> Blood Orange (see food/sweets_and_drinks).
- Matters: Encased Fans (see create_basics/fans); Create Garnished (see food/garnished); Chocolate, Tea and Coffee (see food/sweets_and_drinks).

## glossary/heated | Heated
- Heated: heat level of a fuelled Blaze Burner; recipes marked heated only run in a basin over one; superheated counts too; cools when fuel runs out (see create_basics/heat).
- Matters: Blaze Burner and Heat (see create_basics/heat); Press, Mixer and Basin (see create_basics/processing); TFMG Steel (see industry/tfmg_steel).

## glossary/insulation | Insulation
- Insulation: cold or heat resistance on armor, slows body temperature drift. Sewing Table sews on Goat Fur, Hoglin Hide; hover a piece to read it (see survival/temperature_gear) (see survival/temperature).
- Matters: Insulation and Heaters (see survival/temperature_gear); Temperature (see survival/temperature); Your First Days (see start_here/first_days).

## glossary/jei | JEI
- JEI (Just Enough Items): lists every item and recipe beside inventory. Hover an item, press R for how to make it, U for what it is used in, A to bookmark.
- Machine recipes (mixing, pressing, fan processing) show there too. "Check JEI" in the book means this.
- Matters: Tools of the Trade (see start_here/tools); Core Materials (see create_basics/materials).

## glossary/kinetic_network | Kinetic Network
- Kinetic network: every shaft, gear, belt, machine connected together. Kinetic = Create's word for parts that run on rotation.
- One speed (scaled by gear ratios), one shared stress pool; overstress stops the whole network (see create_basics/rotation) (see create_basics/stress).
- Matters: Rotation (see create_basics/rotation); Stress (see create_basics/stress); Transmission (see create_basics/transmission).

## glossary/mana | Mana
- Mana: what Iron's Spells casts with; refills with time (see exploration/magic). Create: Wizardry makes it a fluid to extract and pipe like water; un-insulated Arcane Pipes leak it (see exploration/create_wizardry).
- Matters: Magic (see exploration/magic); Iron's Spellbooks (see exploration/irons_spellbooks); Create: Wizardry (see exploration/create_wizardry).

## glossary/mb | mB (Millibuckets)
- mB: millibuckets; 1000 mB = one bucket. Create and add-ons measure fluids this way. Goggles show how much a tank holds.
- Examples: Spout fills a terracotta bowl (see survival/thirst); molten steel sets into a Steel Ingot (see industry/tfmg_steel).
- Matters: Fluids, Pipes and Pumps (see create_basics/fluids); Burning Liquid Fuel (see industry/liquid_fuel); TFMG Steel (see industry/tfmg_steel).

## glossary/mechanical_crafting | Mechanical Crafting
- Mechanical crafting: grid of Mechanical Crafters with their paths connected, needs rotation, crafts recipes bigger than 3x3 (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly), e.g. the 5x5 Stellar Core (see stars/stellar_core), Jetpack (see transport/jetpack).
- Fill every slot and it crafts. Press R over the result in JEI to see the layout.

## glossary/mixing | Mixing
- Mixing: Mechanical Mixer stirring a Basin under it (see create_basics/processing). "Heated mixing" = burner under the basin. Brass classic: copper + zinc heated (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly). Alloys: Alloyed (see industry/alloyed).

## glossary/multiblock | Multiblock
- Multiblock: machine of several blocks working as one. Fluid Tanks and Item Vaults join up; a steam boiler is one.
- Others in pack: TFMG's blast furnace, Create Nuclear reactor, Ratatouille's Oven, Modular Accumulator. Shape matters; check Ponder.
- Matters: Fluids, Pipes and Pumps (see create_basics/fluids); TFMG Steel (see industry/tfmg_steel); Nuclear Reactors (see industry/nuclear).

## glossary/overstressed | Overstressed
- Overstressed: machines ask more stress than sources give; the whole network stops (see create_basics/stress) (see create_basics/power_sources).

## glossary/passive_heater | Passive Heater
- Passive heater: heats a Create boiler with no fuel, free but weaker than a Blaze Burner; list of blocks (see create_basics/heat).
- Matters: Blaze Burner and Heat (see create_basics/heat); Heat Is Shared (see connections/heat); Kitchen Automation (see food/kitchen_automation).

## glossary/ponder | Ponder
- Ponder: Create's animated help. Hover almost any Create item (inventory or JEI) and hold W; a scene shows how it works. Many add-ons use it too. First place to look for how a machine behaves.
- Matters: Tools of the Trade (see start_here/tools); Rotation (see create_basics/rotation).

## glossary/purified_water | Purified Water
- Water purity levels, sickness and boiling (see survival/thirst): Dirty, Slightly Dirty, Acceptable, Purified; campfire, furnace, Sand Filter.
- Matters: Thirst (see survival/thirst); Your First Days (see start_here/first_days).

## glossary/redstone_frequency | Redstone Link Frequency
- Redstone Link frequency: two items in its slots; a powered transmitter powers every receiver with that pair, anywhere (see create_basics/redstone_and_control). Also bound by Aeroworks control consoles (see flight/controls) and coaster Restrictor seats (see transport/coasters).

## glossary/rpm | RPM
- RPM (rotations per minute): part speed (see create_basics/rotation). Large to small cogwheel doubles it (see create_basics/transmission). Faster costs more stress (see create_basics/stress). Some machines need a minimum, e.g. 32 RPM for an Alternator (see industry/electricity).

## glossary/saturation | Saturation
- Saturation: hidden buffer from food that empties before the hunger bar starts to drop; AppleSkin shows it (see survival/food_and_health). Thirst's hidden quenched buffer is the same idea (see survival/thirst).
- Matters: Food and Health (see survival/food_and_health); Thirst (see survival/thirst); Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight).

## glossary/sequenced_assembly | Sequenced Assembly
- Sequenced assembly: belt chain of steps (deployers, press), repeated; incomplete item between (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly). Precision Mechanism is the first one. JEI shows each loop.
- Matters: Brass and Assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly); Deployer (see create_basics/deployer); Cataclysm x Create (see exploration/cataclysm_create); Space Metals (see space/space_materials).

## glossary/smog | Smog
- Smog: Create: Smog's air pollution, tracked per chunk; effects and fixes (see survival/pollution) (see survival/pollution_control).

## glossary/su | SU (Stress Units)
- SU (stress units): measure Create power; sources give capacity, machines use impact (see create_basics/stress) (see create_basics/power_sources).
- Matters: Stress (see create_basics/stress); Power Sources (see create_basics/power_sources); Ore Excavation (see industry/ore_excavation).

## glossary/sub_level | Sub-level
- Sub-level: a moving Aeronautics build, a chunk of world with mass; ships, submarines, coaster carts are physics objects (Sable library) (see flight/airships). Aeronautics Compatibility keeps other mods' blocks working (see flight/ship_hardware).
- Matters: Airships (see flight/airships); Building a Ship (see flight/ship_building); Ship Hardware (see flight/ship_hardware); Submarines (see ocean/submarines).

## glossary/super_glue | Super Glue
- Super Glue: bonds blocks into one shape that moves together (see create_basics/contraptions). For ships, Honey Glue does the same job (see flight/ship_building).
- Matters: Contraptions (see create_basics/contraptions); Trains (see transport/trains); Building a Ship (see flight/ship_building); Building a Rocket (see space/rocket_building).

## glossary/superheated | Superheated
- Superheated: stronger Blaze Burner level (blue flame) (see create_basics/heat). Fuels: Blaze Cake, or piped Bioethanol, Peanut Oil, napalm (see industry/liquid_fuel). Counts as heated too; makes twice the smog (see survival/pollution). Create Propulsion's burners can't superheat (see flight/propulsion).
- Matters: Blaze Burner and Heat (see create_basics/heat); Burning Liquid Fuel (see industry/liquid_fuel); Pollution (see survival/pollution).

## glossary/tags | Tags
- Tag: named group of items, fluids or blocks. Recipes and mods check the tag instead of one exact item, so different mods' versions work alike.
- Shared fluid tags let Blaze Burners burn TFMG fuels; the steel tag takes any steel. Glass in the greenhouse glass tag shelters crops.
- Matters: Shared Metals (see materials/shared_metals); Fuel Goes Everywhere (see connections/fuel); Seasons (see survival/seasons).

## glossary/tick | Tick
- Tick: Minecraft's step of game time; 20 ticks = one second at full speed.
- Burn times often in ticks: 24000 ticks is 20 minutes. Chunks that "tick" keep machines running.
- Matters: Burning Liquid Fuel (see industry/liquid_fuel); Chunk Loaders (see industry/power_loader).

## glossary/value_panel | Value Panel
- Value panel (value box): small panel on a machine face; right-click or scroll to change its value (see create_basics/tools_wrench). Used by speed controllers, windmills, gearshifts, chunk loaders (see industry/power_loader).
- Matters: Wrench and Goggles (see create_basics/tools_wrench); Transmission (see create_basics/transmission); Chunk Loaders (see industry/power_loader).

## glossary/washing | Washing
- Washing: fan processing through water (see create_basics/fans); crushed ore -> nuggets + byproduct chance (see create_basics/crushing); also cleans clogged filter cartridges (see survival/pollution_control).
- Matters: Encased Fans (see create_basics/fans); Crushing and Ore Doubling (see create_basics/crushing); Ore Doubling (see materials/ore_doubling); Clearing the Air (see survival/pollution_control).

## glossary/y_level | Y Level
- Y level: height in the world, shown on F3. Lower = deeper; Overworld goes below zero.
- Ore Guide gives ore depths in Y. Water above Y 100 or below Y 48 is one step cleaner. Cold Sweat: Altitude gets colder with Y.
- Matters: Ore Guide (see materials/ore_guide); Your First Days (see start_here/first_days); Create and Altitude Heat (see survival/temperature_create).
