# Food and Farming (food)

## food/first_food | Food in Your First Days
- Food is spread thin: many species, each in its own biome. Entry lists what is easy with stone tools, crafting table, campfire, by area; rare finds marked rare.
- Hunt nearby, cook on campfire. Wild crops/fruit are food in any season; only replanting cares. Raw mushrooms aren't food: make stew. Meat and fish don't quench thirst; fruit does.
- Seasons: world starts early spring. Picked wild crop is food at once; season matters only when planting. In season now: carrots, potatoes, onions, sweet berries, strawberries. Wheat is a summer crop, so seeds from grass grow slowly until then (see survival/seasons_crops).
- Campfire: 3 sticks, coal or charcoal, 3 logs. No coal: smelt a log in a furnace for charcoal. Cooks 4 things at once with no fuel, boils water, keeps you warm. Bowls: 3 planks make 4.
- Board and knife: shows item farmersdelight:cutting_board. Cutting Board (4 planks, 2 sticks) + Flint Knife (flint, stick) slice pineapples, melons, pumpkins and cut meat. Cooking Pot needs iron, so comes later.
- Everywhere: grass drops wheat seeds; short grass also coffee beans and cassava seeds (all three summer crops). Oak leaves drop apples (rare) and acorns. Mushroom stew: a brown + a red mushroom in a bowl, on a crafting table. Zombies sometimes drop onions.
- Fishing: Bamboo Pole [create_galore_fishing:bamboo_pole] (3 bamboo) needs no reel. In inventory right-click it with a String Line (4 string + stick), a hook (iron nuggets) and worms. Hold to cast, strike when float dips, hold to reel. Cook fish on campfire.
  - Warning: pufferfish, lionfish, stonefish, blowfish, boxfish, hagfish, stingrays poison you raw.
  - Tip: no rod? Aquaculture's fish swim in ponds/rivers; hit one and it drops itself, edible even raw.
  - String Line: 4 string and a stick. Hooks: iron nuggets. Worms: hoeing grass turns one up now and then. Each biome has its own fish (see fishing/overview).
- Hydrating food: meat, fish, bread, potatoes give no water. Fruit does. Best: melon, pineapple slices, pears, peaches, oranges. Good: apples, tomatoes, onions, mango. A little: berries, carrots, beetroot. Melon juice (4 slices, sugar, bottle) is a full drink.
- Beach:
  - Wild cabbages and beetroots grow on sand, ~1 patch per 30 chunks; eat as is.
  - Pineapples: cut on board for 6 slices, top drink-food.
  - Wild rice, wild carrots and onions: rarer.
  - Crabs: a claw becomes Raw Crab in the crafting grid.
  - No cows, pigs, sheep spawn on beaches; fish and crabs are your meat. Dried kelp (smelt kelp) weak but endless.
  - Stony shore: wild potatoes, crabs. Snowy beach: cabbages and beetroots too.
  - shows item farmersdelight:wild_cabbages. Cabbage and beetroot are autumn crops: grow slowly if planted in spring.
- Ocean and river: fish with rod or hit Aquaculture fish; Clams (Naturalist) on sea floor give Clam Meat; squid killed with a knife drop tentacles; rivers: ducks, bass, crayfish, salmon. Running river water is one step cleaner than still water.
- Desert: rabbits easy meat; komodo dragons drop bushmeat but bite back; Hamimelons (Fruits Delight) grow only here, break for slices; wild tomatoes rare, few tomatoes; desert villages have wheat farms, water, bread in chests.
  - Tip: deserts count as tropical: summer crops (wheat, melons, tomatoes, sugar cane) grow all year; spring and autumn crops grow slowly.
  - Warning: never rains in a desert; carry water.
- Badlands: thinnest for food: wild tomatoes, komodo dragons, rabbits on edges, whatever you fish. Summer crops grow all year; build farm early or move on.
- Savanna: no cows, pigs, sheep, chickens. Ostriches: drumsticks (best meat here); boars: porkchops; hippos: melon slices but hit hard; giraffes and elephants: bushmeat. Avoid lions. Summer crops grow all year.
- Jungle (richest start): melons grow wild, lots; pigs, cows, sheep, chickens, capybaras (bushmeat); wild rice, cranberry bushes, fruit trees (mango, peach, lychee, durian). Summer crops grow all year. Rice needs the Cooking Pot.
- Plains: cows, sheep, pigs, chickens; hedgehogs, rats, snakes drop morsels; wild carrots and onions ~1 patch per 120 chunks; lemon trees (1 per 32 chunks), rare blueberry and raspberry bushes; villages: bread, potatoes, carrots, farms.
- Meadow: few animals (some sheep, rabbits, hedgehogs). Look for lemon trees, carrots, onions, or go to a plains village.
- Forests: no pigs. Deer (venison), boars (porkchops), turkeys (drumsticks), plus cows, sheep, chickens. Fruit trees in forest and flower forest ~1 in 20 chunks: apple, orange, fig, kiwi. Pear trees in birch forest, 1 in 15 chunks.
  - Dark forest: cranberry bushes; huge mushrooms drop mushrooms for stew; many monsters at night.
  - Cherry grove: weakest; deer main meat, few rabbits and sheep.
  - Warning: bears and tigers drop no food; leave alone.
  - shows item naturalist:venison. Cook meat on campfire: cooked venison and drumsticks fill 6.
- Taiga (one of best starts): sweet berries (common, in season); brown mushrooms everywhere (red rarer); turkeys, cows, sheep, pigs, rabbits; wild potatoes, hawberry trees, blueberry bushes. Bears dangerous, drop only fur.
- Snow (snowy plains, snowy taiga, ice spikes): rabbits, persimmon trees, blueberry bushes, rare sweet berries, fish (break the ice). Snowy biomes grow only winter crops: cabbage, sweet berries, hawberry, kiwi, persimmon. Set up campfire first (cooks + warms): when too cold, part of your hearts freeze and won't heal, so food alone can't patch you up (see survival/temperature).
- Mountains: Grove: deer, boars, persimmon trees. Slopes/peaks: goats (drop chevon), rabbits on slopes; mammoths lots of meat, fight hard. Stony peaks: wild tomatoes. Windswept hills: like plains + wild potatoes.
- Swamp and mangrove: no cows, pigs, sheep, chickens. Ducks, bass, catfish, capybaras; wild rice, blueberry and cranberry bushes; mushrooms of both colors for stew. Swamp water is Dirty: boil it.
- Mushroom fields: Mooshrooms drop beef; milking one with a bowl gives mushroom stew. Fungi Delight's morel and inky cap cows drop beef too. Mushrooms everywhere.
- Caves: lush caves: glow berries hang from ceiling, lots, all year, hydrate a little. Alex's Caves' Candy Cavity full of sweets, but deep and dangerous; not a day-one trip.
- Terralith biomes work like the closest vanilla area (Terralith desert follows desert page, etc.).
- MushroomQuest stumps/logs in forests, taigas, swamps drop dozens of wild mushrooms; some good food, some deadly; read tooltip before eating.
- Fungi Delight morels and inky caps aren't food raw: inky caps cut on board into cleaned caps; morels go into Cooking Pot dishes.
- Patch rarity: wild cabbages and beetroots 1 in 30 chunks, wild rice 1 in 20, carrots and onions 1 in 120, potatoes and tomatoes 1 in 100.
- Related: Your First Days (see start_here/first_days); Thirst (see survival/thirst); Seasonal Crops (see survival/seasons_crops); Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight); Fishing (see fishing/overview); Sea Food (see fishing/sea_food); Animals and Husbandry (see food/animals).

## food/farmers_delight | Farmer's Delight
- The pack's cooking layer: adds crops, a few kitchen blocks, many proper meals. Most other food mods here use its blocks; learn first. Meals give lasting effects, not just hunger points.
- Cooking Pot [farmersdelight:cooking_pot]: shows item farmersdelight:cooking_pot. Combine ingredients into a meal, usually with a bowl. Needs heat source directly below. Meal waits inside until taken with the right container.
- Heat sources: Stove, campfire, fire, lava, magma block, Create's Blaze Burner via Central Kitchen (see food/kitchen_automation).
- Stove [farmersdelight:stove]: shows item farmersdelight:stove. Heat source for pots and skillets. Also a passive heater (see glossary/passive_heater) for a Create boiler (see glossary/boiler). See Heat (see create_basics/heat).
- Skillet [farmersdelight:skillet]: shows item farmersdelight:skillet. Cooks one item at a time on a heat source; also portable cooker: stand next to heat and hold food in other hand.
- Cutting Board: shows item farmersdelight:cutting_board. Put item on board, use right tool: knives cut food, other tools handle wood etc. Fortune on tool raises odds of rare results.
  - Knives also harvest Straw [farmersdelight:straw] from grass, wheat, rice. Flint Knife [farmersdelight:flint_knife] is starter; iron and better follow. Knife used in a crafting grid comes back one point more worn instead of being used up.
  - Wheat + water bucket = Wheat Dough; bucket keeps its water.
- Rich Soil [farmersdelight:rich_soil]: shows item farmersdelight:rich_soil. Organic Compost slowly decays into Rich Soil. Crops and mushrooms on it grow better; never dries out. Create's Irrigation Tower (see food/ratatouille) can turn farmland into it.
- Meal effects: Nourishment stops food meter dropping, except when healing with saturation (see glossary/saturation). Comfort gives natural regeneration however hungry you are. AppleSkin overlay shows how much a food restores.
- Related (add-on foods, machines, crops): Delight Add-ons (see food/farmers_delight_addons); Kitchen Automation (see food/kitchen_automation); Brewing and Fermenting (see food/brewing); Berries and Farm Helpers (see food/crops_and_herbs); Food in Your First Days (see food/first_food).

## food/farmers_delight_addons | Delight Add-ons
- Small mods add foods that all use Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight) pot, board, skillet. Same rules everywhere: bring ingredients, heat source, right container. Look recipes up in JEI (see glossary/jei): hover, press R. Delightful Creators is the Create bridge.
- Sniffers Sniff Delights: shows item farmersdelight:tomato_seeds. Sniffers dig up tomato seeds, cabbage seeds, rice or onions in ~9 of 10 digs; torchflower seeds and pitcher pods are rare now. Those four crops are also sniffer food: lure and breed sniffers with them. Sniffer eggs are vanilla: brush Suspicious Sand in Warm Ocean Ruins. Needs Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight). Makes sniffers dig up tomato, cabbage, rice, onion seeds far more often.
- Extended: shows item farmersdelight_extended:cheeseburger. Dishes made only from existing Minecraft, Create, Farmer's Delight items; no new crops or mobs. Cheese, burgers, breakfast plates, ice cream.
- Fruits Delight: shows item fruitsdelight:pineapple_pie. Fruit trees/bushes (pear, kiwi, mango, persimmon, lychee, fig, more) with jams, pies, jellos, popsicles, drinks. Right-click leaves showing ripe fruit to pick; leaves grow new fruit later.
- Fungi Delight: shows item fungidelight:truffle. Mushroom cooking: morel, inky cap, truffle crops and dishes (truffle pasta, morel soup). Morel cows exist.
- My Nether's Delight: shows item mynethersdelight:hoglin_sausage. Hoglin, ghast, Nether-plant dishes, Nether-flavoured cabinets and decor. For cooking in the Nether. Bastion chests hold some extras, like strider eggs.
- Ocean's Delight: shows item oceansdelight:fugu_slice. Food from sea creatures: fugu, guardian parts, tentacles, more. Warning: cook carefully; fugu is poisonous raw.
- Berries and Cherries Delight: grapes, strawberries, cherries, jams, pies. Pit cherries by crafting with any knife. Its own Iron Knife is merged into Farmer's Delight's; old ones turn into it. Right-click ripe plant to pick (never hurts). Raspberry bushes prick anyone walking through without armor.
- Aquaculture Delight: Aquaculture fish work in Farmer's Delight recipes.
- Delightful Creators: Create bridge: soups as fluids, sequenced assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly) meals, a Mechanical Cutter. See Kitchen Automation (see food/kitchen_automation).
- Brewin' and Chewin': kegs and fermented drinks: Brewing (see food/brewing).
- Base mechanics: Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight).

## food/brewing | Brewing and Fermenting
- Brewin' and Chewin' adds fermenting. A keg takes ingredients + a liquid, slowly turns them into a drink or preserved food. Drinks served in a tankard, give status effects as well as food value.
- Keg [brewinandchewin:keg]: shows item brewinandchewin:keg. Holds ingredients and fluid while fermenting. Output is a bottle or food item. Check JEI (see glossary/jei) for each recipe and its time.
- Tankard [brewinandchewin:tankard]: shows item brewinandchewin:tankard. Drinking vessel; stout, vodka, rum, Pale Jane poured into it. Fermented foods like kimchi come out of the keg directly.
- Create via Central Kitchen: pipes insert fluid into keg and pull it out; funnels extract item product; Spout fills a tankard with drink; Item Drain pours it out. More: Kitchen Automation (see food/kitchen_automation).
- Related: Kitchen Automation (see food/kitchen_automation); Delight Add-ons (see food/farmers_delight_addons); Chocolate, Tea and Coffee (see food/sweets_and_drinks).

## food/kitchen_automation | Kitchen Automation
- Central Kitchen teaches Create to use Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight) blocks. Slice & Dice and Delightful Creators add Create-powered cutting and kitchen recipes. Soups are fluids, so a Spout fills bowls.
- Heat (Central Kitchen): shows item create:blaze_burner. Blaze Burner [create:blaze_burner] under a pot or stove heats it; hotter burner speeds up some utensils. Farmer's Delight heat sources also count as passive boiler heaters (see glossary/passive_heater).
- Cooking Pot: shows item farmersdelight:cooking_pot. Mechanical Arm can place the bowl and take the meal; Funnel can pull it out; Packagers and pipes feed solid and fluid ingredients; Factory Gauges help. See Packages (see create_basics/packages).
- Cutting Board: shows item farmersdelight:cutting_board. Arm can lay an ingredient on board; Deployer holding a knife in use mode cuts it, so can a Mechanical Saw. Stove and Skillet accept ingredients from an Arm too.
- Slicer (Slice & Dice): shows item sliceanddice:slicer. Rotating cutter; right-click with a knife. Runs Farmer's Delight cutting board recipes; also works on a Basin. Needs rotation: Rotation (see create_basics/rotation).
- Sprinkler: shows item sliceanddice:sprinkler. Place below a fluid pipe. Fluid sets effect: Liquid Fertilizer feeds crops, potions affect mobs, lava burns, water moisturises. Not just a farm tool.
  - Liquid Fertilizer is mixed (see glossary/mixing) from tree fertilizer + water, or Organic Compost + water. Filling Organic Compost with it makes Rich Soil in a Spout.
  - Slice & Dice also fills a bottle with Create's chocolate to make Hot Cocoa.
- Mechanical Cutter: shows item delightfulcreators:mechanical_cutter. Delightful Creators: processes cutting board recipes on items beneath it, from depot or belt. Cheaper to set up than a Slicer; same idea. Delightful Creators also makes soups and stews fluids, so Spout can fill bowls.
- Many dishes (steak and potatoes, pies, sandwiches) have Sequenced Assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly) versions; cookies and cakes can be compacted (see glossary/compacting). Look in JEI (see glossary/jei).
- Feasts and pies: an Arm takes servings; last serving stays in place until a fresh whole dish replaces it. Pancake stacks take only matching flavours, up to 12.
- Related (other Create-powered food): Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight); Create Ratatouille (see food/ratatouille); Brewing and Fermenting (see food/brewing); Create Integrated Farming (see food/integrated_farming).

## food/ratatouille | Create Ratatouille
- Farm-to-table Create machines: bulk baking, grain threshing, squeezing, crop ripening, composting. Sits beside Farmer's Delight, doesn't replace it. Soil basics: Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight).
- The pack's only salt, cocoa powder and butter are Ratatouille's.
- Oven: shows item ratatouille:oven. Multiblock (see glossary/multiblock) heated by a Blaze Burner (see create_basics/heat). Oven Fans keep air moving. Better heat, air, space = faster bulk baking and smoking. Goggles (see create_basics/tools_wrench) read its level.
- Thresher: shows item ratatouille:thresher. Processes grain and crops; works well on a belt. Output slides out the side. Recipes under Threshing in JEI (see glossary/jei).
- Squeeze Basin: shows item ratatouille:squeeze_basin. Squeezes and fills. Products leave from the side with a port; also takes fluid. A Deployer can make it faster.
- Spreader: shows item ratatouille:spreader. Ripens crops in an area by spending Ripen Matter. With Mature Matter puts animals in love mode. Speed and a nozzle widen reach.
- Irrigation Tower: shows item ratatouille:irrigation_tower. Keeps farmland in range moist from a water tank. Filled with Compost Tea, slowly turns farmland into Farmer's Delight Rich Soil Farmland.
- Compost Tower composts food waste into compost products. Mechanical Demolder frees items from moulds (chocolate, cake, popsicle). Related: Sweets and Drinks (see food/sweets_and_drinks).
- Salt: shows item ratatouille:salt. The pack's one salt. Heated boil stone makes it; Northstar's brine and basalt recipes now give it too. Leftover Northstar salt converts 1:1 (see glossary/canonical_item) in a crafting grid.
- Cocoa Powder and Butter: shows item ratatouille:cocoa_powder. The pack's one cocoa powder and butter. Ratatouille presses and mills them; Confectionery's chocolate now uses them. Old Confectionery cocoa converts 1:1.

## food/garnished | Create Garnished
- Big food-and-decor addon: nine nuts, glazes and coatings, many Create-style recipes with fans and haunting (see glossary/haunting). Also a large set of coloured building stones.
- Nuts: shows item garnished:cashew_cookie. Pecan, almond, cashew, walnut, peanut, chestnut, hazelnut, pistachio, macadamia. Crushed to cracked nuts, then coated, sweetened or honeyed.
  - Coating mostly Create processing: Deployer applies cinder flour to a nut; compacting (see glossary/compacting) adds effects such as strength to a cinder-coated nut. Garnish Compound and Liquid Garnish finish dishes. Check JEI (see glossary/jei) for each nut's route.
- Fan recipes in two directions: fans behind coloured Liquefied Mastic Resin bulk-dye items in 16 colours; fan behind powdered snow does bulk freezing. Fans (see create_basics/fans) explains how fan catalysts (see glossary/fan_catalyst) work.
- Garnish Compound: shows item garnished:garnish_compound. Key ingredient in finishing dishes. ~47 Garnished recipes run in the Farmer's Delight cooking pot, where many Garnished meals end up. Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight) covers the pot.
- Many haunting recipes: amethyst shards -> amber remnant, ender jelly -> cosmic powder, crestfallen flora -> dragon breath. Zultanite and other cut stones come in many colours for building.

## food/sweets_and_drinks | Chocolate, Tea and Coffee
- Two addons: Create Confectionery (chocolate, candy, gingerbread) and Create Cafe (coffee, tea, syrups). Both lean on Create's mixing (see glossary/mixing), filling, compacting (see glossary/compacting).
- Confectionery: shows item create_confectionery:chocolate_bricks. Chocolate in milk, dark, white, ruby as fluids; compact into bars or build with chocolate bricks. Also candy, caramel, marshmallow, gingerbread.
- More sweets: candy cane tools, Gingerbread Man mob, hot chocolate drinks. White, dark, hot chocolate use Ratatouille (see food/ratatouille)'s cocoa butter and powder. Look recipes up in JEI (see glossary/jei) with R.
- Coffee: fruit crushed to beans, roasted in a smoker, then milled or crushed to grounds. Syrups flavour drinks, applied with a Spout.
- Tea and Syrups: shows item createcafe:strawberry_milk_tea. Mixed over heat from fruit, melted sugar, often milk or water. Fruit from Fruits Delight, Berries and Cherries, Twilight Forest, Northstar all count. Mixing recipes need a Blaze Burner under the mixer. Tapioca flour, oreo, crushed cookies give bubble tea style toppings.

- Heat: Heat (see create_basics/heat). Serving to customers: Cozy Cafe (see food/cozy_cafe).

## food/cozy_cafe | Cozy Cafe
- Open a small restaurant. Customers spawn at a sign, order from your menu, leave tips and dirty plates. Menu items are foods and drinks; menu screen says if one can't be added.
- Cafe Manager [cozycafe:cafe_manager]: shows item cozycafe:cafe_manager. Heart of the cafe. Place Cafe Menus in its working area and link a Cafe Sign before opening. Rename cafe, view stats, see reputation grow.
- Opening: working area is behind the Manager. Cafe closes on its own when a new day starts; open it again each day; a closed cafe gets no customers.
- Cafe Sign [cozycafe:cafe_sign]: shows item cozycafe:cafe_sign. Marks where customers appear. Sneak + right-click the Manager to link it before placing the sign.
- Plating Station [cozycafe:plating_station]: shows item cozycafe:plating_station. For mains. Right-click with a Serving Plate, then a menu item, then empty hand to take the plated dish. Dirty serving plates drop after customers leave; hold use on a sponge or sink to clean.
- Tip: Cafe Catalog [cozycafe:cafe_catalog] in offhand shows menu price and decor info in tooltips.
- Themed decor sets a theme and modifiers (tips, patience, price). Food: Sweets and Drinks (see food/sweets_and_drinks).

## food/crops_and_herbs | Berries and Farm Helpers
- Small mods make growing easier. Berry Good improves berries. Better Farming Right Click saves replanting time. Seasons decide when crops grow. Fruit trees, jams, pies come from Delight add-ons (see food/farmers_delight_addons).
- Berry Good: shows item berry_good:sweet_berry_basket. Makes sweet berries less annoying to eat; adds berry foods: baskets, pips, meatballs, glowgurt, more. Glow berries get same treatment.
- Better Farming Right Click: right-click a mature crop to harvest and replant in one go. Toggle in its in-game options; also a no-cooldown setting for tools and seeds.
- Rice, tomatoes, onions come from Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight).
- Seasons matter: Serene Seasons ties growth to calendar; many crops struggle out of season; plan farms around it. Seasonal Integration extends this to other mods. See Seasons (see survival/seasons). Create farms: Integrated Farming (see food/integrated_farming).

## food/integrated_farming | Create Integrated Farming
- Farming machines for contraptions: Vacuum Harvester, Roosts (poultry), Fishing Nets (fish while moving). Several recipes use Farmer's Delight items.
- Vacuum Harvester: shows item create_integrated_farming:vacuum_harvester. Rotation charges its chamber (30 s at 64 RPM (see glossary/rpm) by default). At full pressure harvests every mature crop around it at once. Take output from any side but the shaft.
  - On a contraption the chamber charges on a timer, even while parked. Each pulse covers whole area around its position. Contraption Controls can pause it. See Contraptions (see create_basics/contraptions).
- Roost: shows item create_integrated_farming:roost. Right-click a bird with an empty Roost to catch it, or lead one in. Poultry produce over time; feeding speeds it up, by hand, belt or Arm.
- Fishing Net: shows item create_integrated_farming:fishing_net. Made from Farmer's Delight's Safety Net. Moved on a contraption it fishes in open water or lava: the fish rods catch there, small ones most; also catches small animals.
- Mechanical Arms can harvest lily pads and powdery crops. A Spout speeds up Organic Compost [farmersdelight:organic_compost] decay, which feeds Farmer's Delight's Rich Soil.
- Animals: Animals (see food/animals). Delight basics: Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight).

## food/bees | Productive Bees
- Dozens of bee species, each with own honeycomb. Resource bees yield metals and gems; many ore bees exist only if the matching ore block does in your pack.
- Advanced Hives: shows item productivebees:advanced_oak_beehive. Better beehive for many bee types. Wild nests spawn in world. Expansion boxes add capacity. Each species has a favourite flower or block.
- Centrifuge: shows item productivebees:centrifuge. Spins combs into items and wax; Powered one runs on power. Metal and gem combs give ore or gems by chance. Alloy, rare-metal, rare-drop combs are off: Balance Changes (see connections/balance).
- Cages and Breeding: shows item productivebees:bee_cage. Catch bees in Bee Cage; breed new ones in Breeding Chamber. Honey Treats help. Breeding recipes in JEI (see glossary/jei). Wild nests spread species too.
- A metal or gem bee exists only if the pack has that material's block: zinc (Create), lead (Create Nuclear), nickel and aluminium (TFMG) bees possible. Lead uses the shared lead tag (see glossary/tags). Ore Guide (see materials/ore_guide).
- Create tie-in: Super Experience bee's comb can be mixed in a superheated (see create_basics/heat) Mixer into a Nugget of Super Experience from Create: Enchantment Industry.
- Power: Electricity (see industry/electricity).

## food/animals | Animals and Husbandry
- Animal Husbandry: animals have hunger, cleanliness, happiness, health, inherited traits (genes); care changes what you get. Check them in the Farm Ledger. Brush for happiness; sick ones need Animal Medicine.
- Farm Ledger: shows item animalhusbandry:farm_ledger. Tracks hunger, cleanliness, growth, yield, fertility, happiness. Farm Magnifying Glass [animalhusbandry:magnifying_glass] inspects one animal: sickness, cooldowns, wool, milk; shows if male or female.
- Grooming: shows item animalhusbandry:brush. Brush animals to raise happiness. Feeding Troughs hold feed; Brushing Posts help grooming; sick animals need Animal Medicine [animalhusbandry:animal_medicine]. Fertility Potion [animalhusbandry:fertility_potion] readies a pair to mate (use on each for a pair right now).
- Traits (see in ledger): Glutton grows fast; Truffle Snout finds more truffles; Heavyweight drops double meat and leather.
- Eggs and Cheese: shows item animalhusbandry:chicken_nest. Chicken Nests hold eggs. Milk in a cauldron becomes cheese blocks and slices. Pigs with the right trait dig up truffles; either truffle (this one or Fungi Delight's) works in Animal Medicine and Fungi Delight dishes.
- Create tie-ins: Roosts and Fishing Nets from Integrated Farming (see food/integrated_farming) handle poultry on contraptions; Ratatouille (see food/ratatouille)'s Spreader breeds animals. Bees: Productive Bees (see food/bees).
- Pack runs Animal Husbandry 0.4.2: no rabbit genetics, no Angora armor.
- Wild animals never get hungry or sick; care needs start once domesticated.
- Gestation: mother carries young 3 days (cow), 2 days (sheep, goat), 1.5 days (pig) before birth; breeding isn't instant.
- Taming and breeding: cows, pigs, sheep, goats, chickens don't breed from wheat or carrots. First food handed to a wild one tames it; after that food only fills it, once a day. Tamed pairs breed by themselves when both healthy, happy (80 or more), male and female, within 8 blocks.
  - Happiness starts at 30. Day with sky or light 8+ adds 5; brushing adds 10 (once a day); grooming in last 3 days adds 3. Fed and brushed daily, a pair is ready in ~3 days.
- Sickness: each day a tamed animal may fall sick if unhappy (under 50), unfed for more than 3 days, crowded (over 5 animals within 4 blocks), or kept dark (no sky and light under 4). Torch-lit barn is fine. After 4 sick days it loses a heart a day and can die. Animal Medicine cures it.
- Troughs and eggs: Feeding Trough holds feed or water, not both; tamed animals walk to it when hungry. Only hens lay; Broody hens never do. Egg laid on stone or planks breaks 4 times in 5; on dirt, sand, hay, wool, leaves 1 in 10. Chicken Nest keeps them all.
- Milk: wild cows milk as usual. Tamed cow gives milk only if female and not sick, a few buckets a day.
- Finding sheep: shows item minecraft:white_wool. Sheep live in plains, meadows, cherry groves, forests (birch, dark, flower), taiga, groves, windswept hills, jungles. Terralith lands with sheep: highlands, shrublands, steppe, valleys, spires, snowy taiga. No sheep in savanna, swamp, desert, badlands, snowy plains, beaches, mushroom fields, oceans. Animals mostly appear when land is first generated: look in unvisited land.
- Wool without sheep: 4 string craft 1 white wool (spiders drop string; cobwebs give it to a sword or shears); Shepherd villagers sell wool for an emerald from Apprentice on; four Alex's Caves Dark Tatters make black wool; Bulk Washing (Encased Fan through water) turns dyed wool white.
- Naturalist's small animals (snails, rats, moles, hedgehogs, snakes, birds) and other mods' critters share the spawn pool with farm animals. Sheep have extra spawns of their own, so every biome with sheep has them about as often as vanilla.
- Other animal mods: Critters and Companions: small creatures (red pandas, ferrets, koi, snails, dragonflies); every snail and dragonfly can be tamed (see exploration/creatures); its otter, Dumbo Octopus, Sea Bunny spawns are off. Naturalist: bears, deer, elephants, lions, more; nine of its sea animals are off, see Sea Life (see ocean/sea_life). Sniffers Sniff Delights gives sniffers a job: see Delight Add-ons (see food/farmers_delight_addons).
