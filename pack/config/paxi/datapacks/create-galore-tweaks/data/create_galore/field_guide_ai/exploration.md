# Exploration and Adventure (exploration)

## exploration/dimensions | Dimensions
- Map of places beyond the Overworld and how to reach each; each has own rules, materials, bosses
- The Nether (see exploration/nether): reworked by Incendium
- The End (see exploration/the_end): BetterEnd and YUNG's island
- The Aether (see exploration/aether): glowstone portal
- Twilight Forest (see exploration/twilight_forest): flower pool and a diamond
- Create Deep Seas has an abyss dimension, off in normal play (see ocean/submarines)
- Space is separate: Create: Northstar adds rockets and planets (see space/reaching_space) (see space/planets). Rockets or an Aeronautics ship with a **Dimensional Drive** (from the Northstar-Aeronautics bridge); both need a Stellar Core. Space is the pack's capstone (see stars/why)
- Dimensional Drive [cnbridge:dimensional_drive]: place on an Aeronautics ship, pick a Northstar planet, arm it, fly to the trigger altitude. Bridge lists Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury, Earth Orbit as destinations. Needs a Stellar Core like every way into space (see stars/why). Airships (see flight/airships), Destinations (see flight/destinations)
- Worldgen mods changing the Overworld (Terralith, Tectonic, Alex's Caves): (see exploration/worldgen). Structures on the way: (see exploration/structures)
- Go to: (see exploration/nether) (see exploration/the_end) (see exploration/aether) (see exploration/twilight_forest) (see space/planets)

## exploration/nether | The Nether
- **Incendium** rebuilds the Nether: new biomes, mobs, structures, loot; plays differently from vanilla. Only reshapes newly generated Nether
- Biomes: Infernal Dunes, Inverted Forest, Quartz Flats, Toxic Heap, Volcanic Deltas, Weeping Valley, Ash Barrens, Withered Forest
- Structures: Forbidden Castle, Infernal Altar, Nether Reactor, Piglin Village, Ruined Lab, Sanctum, Quartz Kitchen, Abandoned Tower, Pipeline
- Castles, Sanctum, Nether Reactor guarded by named mobs (knights, archers, pyros, cultists, apostles, more); Piglin villages have civilians and guards
- Structure chests and some mobs hold **artifacts**: unique named weapons/tools (Ragnarok bow, Firestorm crossbow, Trailblazer, Voltaic Trident)
- Castle Paratroopers don't drop wings
- Blaze Burner [create:blaze_burner]: Create's heat source; loaded with a blaze from a Nether fortress spawner. Create's heat (see create_basics/heat) starts here
- Other mods: Cataclysm (see exploration/cataclysm) Nether bosses and arenas; Dungeons and Taverns (see exploration/structure_packs) Nether keeps, ports, skeleton towers
- Nether ores: Ore Guide (see materials/ore_guide)

## exploration/the_end | The End
- **YUNG's Better End Island** redesigns the central island (Ender Dragon fight). **BetterEnd** fills outer islands with new biomes, plants, ores, tools
- Outer biomes: Amber Land, Blossoming Spires, Chorus Forest, Crystal Mountains, Dragon Graveyards, Foggy Mushroomland, Glowing Grasslands, Lantern Woods, Megalake, Neon Oasis, Painted Mountains, Shadow Forest, Sulfur Springs, Umbra Valley, Umbrella Jungle
- End Stone Smelter [betterend:end_stone_smelter]: BetterEnd's furnace for its alloys
- Aeternium Block [betterend:aeternium_block]: BetterEnd's endgame metal, for tools and armor
- Cataclysm adds End structure Ruined Citadel with own bosses (see exploration/cataclysm). Dungeons and Taverns adds End castles and ships (see exploration/structure_packs)

## exploration/aether | The Aether
- Dimension of floating islands above a sea of clouds; own mobs, materials, three dungeons
- Portal: build a frame of **Glowstone**, place water in it; portal swirls blue. Mod can give new players a portal frame item, set by config (see glossary/config)
- Aether Portal Frame [aether:aether_portal_frame]: glowstone frame plus water makes the portal
- Holystone [aether:holystone]: main stone. Dungeons use Carved, Angelic, Hellfire stone; need Bronze, Silver, Gold keys
- Zanite Gemstone [aether:zanite_gemstone]: tool/armor gem; Gravitite and Valkyrie gear come later
- Ambrosium Shard [aether:ambrosium_shard]: lights Aether furnaces, heals. Golden Amber from golden oak trees
- Dungeons (Bronze, Silver, Gold) hold keys and a boss each: Slider, Valkyrie Queen, Sun Spirit
- Parachutes make falling off islands survivable. Cold Parachute [aether:cold_parachute]; Golden Parachute [aether:golden_parachute] is sturdier
- Aether gear stacks with other mods. Invisibility Cloak [aether:invisibility_cloak] toggles with **=** by default here
- Bosses (see exploration/bosses); travel (see exploration/dimensions)

## exploration/twilight_forest | Twilight Forest
- Dark layered dimension of dense forest; eight bosses, own metals and magic items. Added by hand to this pack
- Enter: dig a small pool, ring it with flowers, throw a **Diamond** into the water; lightning strikes and portal opens
- Ironwood Ingot [twilightforest:ironwood_ingot]: first metal; Steeleaf, Knightmetal, Fiery metal follow
- Block of Knightmetal [twilightforest:knightmetal_block]: later metal, tougher than Ironwood and Steeleaf
- Fiery Ingot [twilightforest:fiery_ingot]: from Hydra's Fiery Blood; Fiery tools set targets on fire
- Uncrafting Table [twilightforest:uncrafting_table]: **can't uncraft**; table still repairs and does its own special recipes
- Magic Map [twilightforest:filled_magic_map]: maps the forest. Charms of Keeping [twilightforest:charm_of_keeping_1] and Life also exist
- Bosses in usual order: Naga, Lich, Minoshroom, Hydra, Knight Phantoms, Ur-Ghast, Alpha Yeti, Snow Queen. Each leaves a trophy. Boss Compasses (see stars/boss_compasses) find first four lairs. (see exploration/bosses)
- Lich Trophy [twilightforest:lich_trophy]: one of eight trophies; bosses have spawner blocks in lairs

## exploration/worldgen | World Generation
- Three mods reshape the Overworld (land and caves differ from vanilla before structures); invisible in inventory, met as scenery
- Terralith [shown with minecraft:oak_sapling]: almost 100 new biomes, realistic-to-light-fantasy; only vanilla blocks
- Tectonic [shown with minecraft:stone]: rewrites terrain shaping, taller/grander/more varied; works alongside Terralith
- YUNG's Better Caves [shown with minecraft:deepslate]: full cave generation overhaul; caves differ at every depth
- Tip: these change where things are; check Ore Guide (see materials/ore_guide) before assuming vanilla heights
- Ocean depth matters for submarines (see ocean/submarines): bigger terrain, more places to dive
- Lithostitched and Noisium are libraries, no content. Underground biomes with own rules: Alex's Caves (see exploration/alexs_caves)

## exploration/alexs_caves | Alex's Caves
- Six huge, rare underground biomes with own mobs, blocks, hazards. A **Cave Biome Map** finds the nearest
- Toxic Caves' Acid and Purple Soda are **bottomless** fluids
- Off here: Subterranodon; crafting the small Submarine
- Biomes: **Magnetic Caves**; **Primordial Caves** (dinosaurs, minus rideable Subterranodon); **Toxic Caves** (acid, uranium); **Abyssal Chasm** (deep water trench); **Forlorn Hollows**; **Candy Cavity**
- Location: Primordial Caves under fairly inland ground; Magnetic Caves, Toxic Caves, Forlorn Hollows, Candy Cavity under the most inland ground; Abyssal Chasm under deep ocean
- None starts within 400 blocks of world spawn (Toxic Caves and Forlorn Hollows: 650). Nearest of each is a few thousand blocks away; craft a Cave Biome Map first
- Spelunkery Table [alexscaves:spelunkery_table]: where Cave Tablets [alexscaves:cave_tablet] are studied; place tablet in slot to begin research
- Cave Biome Map [alexscaves:cave_map]: points to nearest cave biome of a type; if none near, travel far and retry
- Toxic Caves are the Create-connected one: Acid and Purple Soda are marked in Create's data as **bottomless** fluids (like water and lava), so a hose pulley draws them without running dry (see create_basics/fluids)
- Uranium Rod [alexscaves:uranium_rod]: Alex's Caves has own uranium and a Nuclear Furnace; separate from Create Nuclear, check JEI (see glossary/jei) before mixing
- Magnetic Caves add Magnetic Levitation Rail and Neodymium nodes
- Abyssal Chasm is best reached in a Deep Seas (see ocean/submarines) submarine
- Not in this world (two rides would skip the vehicle game): **Subterranodon** never spawns; **Submarine**: Hullbreaker no longer drops the Enigmatic Engine so the small sub can't be built; wrecks in chasm ruins can still be fixed. (see transport/getting_around)
- Alex's Caves sulfur dust is not its own item here: its recipes (Slam, Spelunkie, Polymer Plate, gunpowder) use TFMG's sulfur dust; leftover Alex's dust converts 1:1 (see glossary/canonical_item) in a crafting grid

## exploration/structures | Structures
- Guide to which structure mod adds what: villages, ruins, dungeons, forts, wrecks, much loot
- Spacing: surface holds about 6 per km² (one every 400 blocks or so), shared evenly by kind (mod with ten building kinds turns up 5x as often as one with two); next of same kind can be kilometres away
- Below ground roughly one per 250 by 250 blocks, plus mineshafts; a few hundred blocks of open ocean usually hold several sea-floor places. Sky holds about 3 per km². Boss lairs and best-loot dungeons stay rarer than everyday builds
- Left as common as mods make them (part of the land): Ice Maze ice arches, Abyssal Chasm, Toxic Caves acid pits, wild cherry trees, berry bushes
- Structure Packs (see exploration/structure_packs): Towns and Towers, Dungeons and Taverns, When Dungeons Arise, YUNG's
- Create Structures (see exploration/create_structures): buildings with trains, airships, cranes, oil rigs
- Lootr (see exploration/lootr): why a looted chest still has loot for you
- Sky structures: Destinations (see flight/destinations). Sea structures: Underwater Places (see ocean/underwater_places). Bosses defend many: (see exploration/bosses)

## exploration/structure_packs | Structure Packs
- No setup: walk, ride or fly and you find them. Most chests follow Lootr (see exploration/lootr) per-player loot rules
- **Towns and Towers**: many new village styles, pillager outposts, wandering trader camp, ships; biome variants
- **Dungeons and Taverns**: hamlets, taverns for every wood, wells, crypts, shrines, firewatch towers, Nether and End builds (Nether keeps and ports; End castles, ships, lighthouses)
- **When Dungeons Arise**: big roguelike builds: coliseum, aviary, bandit towers, foundry, illager forts, monasteries, shiraz palace, plague asylum, typhon, thornborn towers, mechanical nest; several have bosses
- **YUNG's** reworks vanilla structures: Better Dungeons, Mineshafts, Strongholds, Desert Temples, Jungle Temples, Witch Huts, Ocean Monuments; same places, much better builds and loot
- Dungeon Loot (chest [minecraft:chest]): chests are the reason to visit; Lootr makes them per-player
- Underground builds spread to roughly **one per 250 by 250 blocks**. Every kind still generates, common ones spaced out. Mineshafts, Trial Chambers, buried treasure keep usual rate
- Related: (see exploration/structures) (see exploration/lootr) (see exploration/create_structures)

## exploration/create_structures | Create Structures
- Two Create-themed building mods; chests drop Create components; no special trigger
- **Create: Structures Arise**: mine train, lost train station, pillager steampunk airship, air drop, mini sky village, crusher crane, fluid tank house, witch hut, bastion, ruined castle
- Train Track [create:track]: includes Mine Train and Lost Train Station; see Trains. Ideas: trains (see transport/trains) at mine train/station; airships (see flight/airships) at sky village/steampunk airship. Sky structures: Destinations (see flight/destinations)
- **Create: Let The Adventure Begin**: 62 structures with new loot: railroad, oil rig, quarry, bunker, castle, kings castle, houses in several styles
- Brass [create:brass_ingot]: Let The Adventure Begin adds Create items to loot, so runs feed the metals Create uses
- Oil rig worth a look for oil (see industry/tfmg_oil) fans. Chest loot rules (see exploration/lootr)

## exploration/lootr | Lootr
- Makes every loot container unique per player; two players opening the same dungeon chest each get full loot
- Loot Chest [lootr:lootr_chest]: generated chests turn into these; each player sees/empties only own copy
- Loot Barrel [lootr:lootr_barrel]: barrels, shulkers, decorated pots in structures follow same rule
- Player-placed containers are normal. Only containers that would roll a vanilla loot table become Lootr, including structure packs (see exploration/structure_packs), Create structures (see exploration/create_structures), undersea wrecks (see ocean/underwater_places)
- Suspicious Sand [lootr:suspicious_sand]: archaeology blocks covered too

- Large hard bosses guarding own structures, unique weapons/armor/materials; for well-geared players
- Bosses: Netherite Monstrosity, Ignis, Harbinger, Leviathan, Scylla, Ancient Remnant, Maledictus, Ender Guardian, Ender Golem
- Structures: **Sunken City** deep ocean (good submarine (see ocean/submarines) trip); **Burning Arena** Nether; **Ruined Citadel** End highlands and midlands; **Frosted Prison** snowy plains; **Cursed Pyramid** desert; **Ancient Factory**
- Watch out: mod's own message says the Leviathan is invincible outside water; fight it wet
- Boss health (2 health = 1 heart): Netherite Monstrosity **420**, Ignis **315**, Ancient Remnant **315**, Maledictus **294**, Leviathan **280**, Harbinger **273**, Scylla **273**, Ender Guardian **233**
- Ability keys set in this pack: **Home** general ability; **End** boots; **Insert** helmet; **Delete** chestplate. Change under Controls, Cataclysm
- Boss gear is made with Create machines here (see exploration/cataclysm_create); other bosses (see exploration/bosses)
- Ignitium Ingot [cataclysm:ignitium_ingot]: boss material; Ignitium, Cursium, Black Steel each make a gear line

## exploration/cataclysm_create | Cataclysm x Create
- **Cataclysm x Create Integration** is a data pack (see glossary/datapack): gives Cataclysm gear Create recipes; boss loot turned into equipment by machines, not at a crafting table
- About 35 items made by Sequenced Assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly): base item goes through Deployer, Press, Deployer steps with boss materials. A few use Mechanical Crafting (see glossary/mechanical_crafting)
- Covered: weapons, armor, shields, smithing templates, summoning Eyes
- Mechanical Fusion Anvil [cataclysm:mechanical_fusion_anvil]: made from an Anvil with Sturdy Sheets, Precision Mechanisms, Redstone Blocks
- Example: The Incinerator [cataclysm:the_incinerator]: starts as a Netherite sword; check JEI (see glossary/jei) for its Sequenced Assembly
- Laser Gatling [cataclysm:laser_gatling]: starts as a crossbow, uses electron tubes and redstone blocks
- Also crushing recipes recycling Cataclysm gear: black steel tools, Cursium, Ignitium and more into a Crushing Wheel give nuggets, some ingots, XP nuggets. Read Crushing (see create_basics/crushing), Assembly (see create_basics/brass_and_assembly)
- **Abyssal Sacrifice** (Cataclysm ritual item) is now a compacting (see glossary/compacting) recipe needing a heated Basin, so a Blaze Burner. Heat (see create_basics/heat)
- Links: Cataclysm (see exploration/cataclysm)

## exploration/magic | Magic
- Two mods: Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks (see exploration/irons_spellbooks) full spellcasting; Create: Wizardry (see exploration/create_wizardry) bridges to Create machines with a new fluid, mana, and pipes
- Magic uses mana (see glossary/mana), refills with time
- Spells cast from equipped spell books and scrolls; schools: fire, ice, lightning, holy, ender, blood, evocation, nature. Armor, rings, staffs boost them
- Scroll [irons_spellbooks:scroll]: one-use spell; made at a Scroll Forge or found in loot
- Guides: (see exploration/irons_spellbooks) (see exploration/create_wizardry) (see exploration/bosses) (see exploration/gear)

## exploration/irons_spellbooks | Iron's Spellbooks
- Put spells in a spell book, cast with mana (see glossary/mana); better books hold more slots. Spells belong to schools; gear raises power of one school or all
- **Scrolls**: single-use, anyone can cast, no book needed; spells made/found at several rarity tiers
- Scroll Forge makes scrolls; Inscription Table fills books
- Off here: Portal, Recall, Angel Wings
- Scroll [irons_spellbooks:scroll]
- Spell Books [irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book]: Flimsy Journal (copper) up to Ancient Codex (netherite)
- Scroll Forge [irons_spellbooks:scroll_forge]: where scrolls are made; polished deepslate and crying obsidian
- Inscription Table [irons_spellbooks:inscription_table]: writes spells into a spell book
- Crafting uses: ink in five rarities, Arcane Essence, Mithril, Pyrium and Arcane Ingots, Upgrade Orbs (boost a school). Helpers: Alchemist Cauldron, Arcane Anvil, Pedestals. Rings, necklaces and the spell book are worn in the Accessories screen (see exploration/gear)
- World: wizard towers and a lost Citadel, defended by mage mobs: Pyromancer Tower, Archevoker's Evoker Fort, Ice Spider Den, Ancient Battleground, Catacombs, the Citadel (a Citadel Map exists). Bosses (see exploration/bosses)
- Not in this world: three spells would replace vehicles; never in loot, Scroll Forge or JEI (see glossary/jei): **Portal** (permanent two-way links), **Recall** (home from anywhere), **Angel Wings** (Elytra flight). Portal Frames and Hither-Thither Wand can't be crafted (see transport/getting_around)
- Create hooks in: Inks, Blood Vials, Timeless Slurry, several elixirs have Create filling/emptying recipes; Spout fills from fluid, Drain empties (see create_basics/fluids)
- See also: (see exploration/create_wizardry) (see exploration/magic)

## exploration/create_wizardry | Create: Wizardry
- Makes mana (see glossary/mana) a Create fluid; needs Iron's Spells; machines extract, pipe, use mana. Pipe like water, mind the leaks
- Mana Siphon [create_wizardry:mana_siphon]: draws mana from players, spells, mobs. A crystalline block placed above it can crystallize mana into Arcane Essence. Drained arcane ingots never turn into netherite here
- Arcane Pipe [create_wizardry:arcane_pipe]: carries mana. Watch out: un-insulated pipes rapidly leak it; insulate with Arcane Sheets
- Arcane Pump [create_wizardry:arcane_pump]: Arcane Pipe + cogwheel; moves mana by rotation like a Mechanical Pump
- Arcane Casing [create_wizardry:arcane_casing]: casing (see glossary/encased) for the mod's machines; see Casings in Create entries
- Channeler [create_wizardry:channeler]: harvests Liquid Lightning from lightning bolts; takes a Bottle of Lightning from Iron's Spells
- Blaze Caster [create_wizardry:blaze_caster]: contraption that casts scroll spells; must be superheated (see glossary/superheated); Impulse or Sentry modes. Holds a scroll, casts using mana; not every spell compatible. Heat is Create's (see create_basics/heat), blaze burner
- Other fluids: Liquid Lightning, Blood (compressing meat or boiling mobs), Fire Ale, Netherward Tincture. Several made by Create processing (see create_basics/processing) such as mixing (see glossary/mixing); look up in JEI (see glossary/jei). Back to Magic (see exploration/magic)

## exploration/mushroom_quest | Mushroom Quest
- Foraging mod: dozens of wild mushrooms, giant logs and stumps with mushrooms, cooking. Some food, some medicine, some poison
- Mycopedia [mushroomquest:mycopedia]: field guide to identify mushrooms and learn rarity
- Watch out: eating the wrong one can poison you; a fungal disease exists. Advancements mention medicinal, poisonous, deadly types; identify before eating
- Cooking: campfire, smoking, smelting, shapeless recipes
- Miso Soup [mushroomquest:miso_soup_item]: cooked dishes turn mushrooms into safer food
- Enoki [mushroomquest:enoki]: one of many wild mushrooms
- Good match for Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight)-style cooking, which also has mushroom crops (Fungi Delight). A Witchy Cottage structure exists

## exploration/creatures | Creatures
- Two vanilla-style animal mods: no bosses, no new dimension, more to see and sometimes tame
- **Critters and Companions**: dragonflies (with armor), ferrets, koi, red pandas, jumping spiders, ladybugs, leaf insects, snails, more; plus Grappling Hook and Pearl Necklaces. Its otter, Dumbo Octopus, Sea Bunny spawns are **off** (Hybrid Aquatic has those)
- Koi Fish [crittersandcompanions:koi_fish]: pond fish, carryable in a bucket
- Grappling Hook [crittersandcompanions:grappling_hook]
- **Naturalist**: land animals: bears, deer, boar, lions, tigers, zebras, giraffes, elephants, hippos, rhinos, lizards, snakes, tortoises, ducks, turkeys, vultures, more. Nine sea animals **off**: anglerfish, blobfish, giant isopod, great white shark, piranha, jellyfish, starfish, crab, ray
- Capture Net [naturalist:capture_net]: catches small creatures like butterflies and firefly
- All **snails** and **dragonflies** are Critters and Companions'; every one can be tamed, 1 try in 10: snails with leaves, dragonflies with a spider eye. Snails drop snail shells and slime balls
- Snail Slime [crittersandcompanions:snail_slime_bottle]: glass bottle on any grown snail (Critters' snail, Hybrid Aquatic's scalyfoot snail, the Moon's snail); each snail refills every 5 minutes; a bottle crafts into a slime ball
- Hybrid Aquatic's **sea slug** fills a bottle with **Sea Bunny Slime** (same 5 minutes); four make a Sea Bunny Slime Block. Hand a Critters **clam** (river fishing treasure) to a Hybrid Aquatic **otter**: it cracks it, a **pearl** 1 time in 4, for Pearl Necklaces. Frogs eat wild dragonflies and leave **froglass**, never tamed ones
- Twilight Forest animals: **deer** drop venison, leather and antlers like Overworld deer (venison jerky dries from any venison); **boars** drop porkchops, fat and hide; **helmet crabs** drop raw crab and armor shards
- Sea creatures: Sea Life (see ocean/sea_life). Creature drops feed cooking in Farmer's Delight (see food/farmers_delight)
